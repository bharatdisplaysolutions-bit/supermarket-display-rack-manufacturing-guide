<h2>Why Display Rack Quality Matters</h2>
<p>
  A display rack carries product weight every day. A poorly made rack sags, rusts or wobbles, and
  stores end up replacing it early. The right rack depends on the product weight, aisle width and
  store layout, so these should be decided before ordering.
</p>

<h2>How to Choose a Display Rack</h2>
<ul>
  <li>Ask for the sheet thickness and the load capacity per shelf.</li>
  <li>Check the coating or paint finish and how the surface is prepared before coating.</li>
  <li>Inspect weld points and edge finishing.</li>
  <li>Match rack height and depth to your aisle width and the weight of your products.</li>
  <li>Get the warranty and after-sales terms in writing.</li>
</ul>
